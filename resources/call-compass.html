THE CALL COMPASS

Your map for the intro call. Follow the arrows. They talk 70%, you talk 30%.
The intro call has ONE job: find the real problem and book the demo. You do not pitch here.
1

Small Talk → lower the guard

30 to 60 sec

Where are you based? How's the day? Keep it short, then take the wheel: "Cool if we dive in?"

2

Set the Agenda → take control

Get the yes

"I'll ask a few questions to see if I can actually help. If I can, we explore it. Sound good?" Wait for the yes.

3

Current Situation → get the numbers

Write it down

Why this call? How are you getting customers now? How many a month? Capacity? Revenue? At 100% you'd be at ___?

4

Desired Situation → find the why

Their real WHY

Where do you want to be in 12 months? Revenue, team, your time? "Why not just stay where you are?" Then shut up.

5

Burn the Bridge → more pain

Listen: can't / faster / no time

Play back the gap: you're here, you want there, for [their why]. "What's stopping you from doing this on your own?"

6

Expose the Past → kill objections early

Pre-handle

What have you tried? Did it work? If yes: why not do more? If no: why did it fail? This stops "why haven't you fixed it" later.

7

Summarize + Frame → get "yes, that's right"

Lock alignment

Play their whole situation back. Wait for "yes, that's right." Then: "So the one thing that gets you there is ___, agree?"

8

Position + Book the Demo → the handoff

Book call 2. Do NOT pitch yet.

"Based on what I'm hearing, I know exactly how to help." Then STOP, let them ask how. One line on what you do, then: "This is easier to show than tell. Next step is a quick demo call." Lock the time now. You do NOT pitch here. The demo call is where you pitch.

Locking the demo (the real close of this call)
  • Assume it. Offer two times, not "do you want to."
  • Set the frame: 30 to 60 min, computer + quiet room, all decision-makers there.
  • Answer their questions with questions. Stay in control.
  • Record every call with Fathom. Review one a week.
The one objection killer
"What makes you say that?"
Then let them talk themselves through it. Most objections dissolve when said out loud. Don't argue. Ask.
